About Delta Chi

A brotherhood built to last.

Delta Chi first came to Purdue in 1927 and returned to campus in 2024. Today, brothers volunteer in Greater Lafayette, raise funds for the V Foundation, and help each other make the most of their time at Purdue.

Our four pillars

  • Promote friendship
  • Develop character
  • Advance justice
  • Support a sound education
